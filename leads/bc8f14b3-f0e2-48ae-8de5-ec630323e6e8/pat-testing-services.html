Common Questions

Essential information about workplace electrical safety.

How often should testing be carried out? expand_more
Testing frequency depends on the environment and the equipment type. Office equipment typically requires testing every 12-24 months, while industrial tools on construction sites may need testing every 3 months. Our engineers provide a tailored risk assessment for your site.
Is PAT testing a legal requirement? expand_more
While the specific term "PAT Testing" isn't in law, the Health & Safety at Work Act (1974) and Electricity at Work Regulations (1989) require employers to maintain electrical systems in a safe condition. Regular testing is the industry-standard way to demonstrate compliance.
Will testing disrupt my staff? expand_more
We aim for zero downtime. Each test takes approximately 3-5 minutes per item. For critical servers or essential machinery, we can arrange out-of-hours testing to ensure your business remains operational without interruption.
