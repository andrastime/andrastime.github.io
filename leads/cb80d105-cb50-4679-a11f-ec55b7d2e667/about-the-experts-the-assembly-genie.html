What started as a one-man mission in Birmingham to help neighbors with IKEA wardrobes has evolved into the region's premier assembly collective. Our "magic" isn't supernatural—it's the result of technical precision, years of experience, and a genuine passion for detail.

At The Assembly Genie, we understand that your furniture isn't just wood and screws; it's the backdrop to your life. We treat every home with the utmost respect, ensuring a clean, quiet, and masterfully executed service every single time.
