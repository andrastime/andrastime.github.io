Common Questions

Expert advice on your home electrical systems.

How often should I have an EICR inspection?

expand_more
For domestic homeowners, an Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) is recommended every 10 years. For rented properties, it is a legal requirement every 5 years or at the change of tenancy.

What are the signs that my house needs a rewire?

expand_more
Common signs include frequently blown fuses, flickering lights, burning smells from outlets, or having an older consumer unit with wooden backing or round pin sockets.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency call-outs?

expand_more
Yes, we provide emergency electrical services across North and North West London. If you have a total power loss or smell burning, contact us immediately.
