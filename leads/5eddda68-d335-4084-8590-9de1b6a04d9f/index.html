The Legacy

A Century of Current

1912

The Spark on Albion Street

Founded in a small warehouse in Leeds, Albion Electrical began by supplying vital components to the booming local textile industry as it transitioned to electric power.

1954

Post-War Expansion

As rebuilding efforts swept the nation, Albion scaled its operations, establishing a regional distribution network that prioritized speed and massive inventory reserves to keep the trade moving.

1998

The Digital Shift

Embracing the future, Albion modernized its supply chain with early adoption of computerized inventory systems, ensuring the 'best products at competitive prices' mandate could scale nationally.

Present Day

Modern Performance

Today, Albion stands as a premier technical wholesaler, merging our heritage of personal service with cutting-edge logistics to power the modern infrastructure of tomorrow.
