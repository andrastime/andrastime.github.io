Common Questions

Is PAT testing a legal requirement? expand_more
While PAT testing itself isn't explicitly named as a legal requirement, the Electricity at Work Regulations (1989) state that any electrical equipment that has the potential to cause injury must be maintained in a safe condition. PAT testing is the most widely recognized method for demonstrating compliance with health and safety laws.
How much does PAT testing cost? expand_more
Pricing typically depends on the volume of items. We offer a tiered pricing structure starting from a flat call-out fee including the first 20 items, followed by a low per-item rate for larger offices or industrial sites. Contact us for a bespoke "Voltage-Ready" quote.
How often should equipment be tested? expand_more
Frequency depends on the equipment type and environment. Construction site tools usually require 3-month testing, while office desktop computers may only need testing every 2 to 4 years. We provide a full risk-based schedule after our initial assessment.
What happens if an item fails? expand_more
If an item fails, we immediately label it as "DANGER: DO NOT USE" and isolate it from the power supply. Minor repairs like fuse replacements or plug rewires are often completed on-site for free to help you stay operational.
