Reliable Service You Can Trust

IMAA Plumbing & Heating is a family-run business built on the foundation of technical excellence and honest advice. We began as a small local team in London and have grown through word-of-mouth recommendations.

Our mission is to provide more than just a fix; we provide peace of mind. Every one of our engineers is fully Gas Safe registered and undergoes continuous training on the latest high-efficiency heating systems. Whether it's a minor leak or a complex boiler installation, we treat your home with the same respect as our own.
