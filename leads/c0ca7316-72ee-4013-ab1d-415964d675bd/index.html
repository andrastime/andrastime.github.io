About Paul Wildbore

Hi, I’m Paul Wildbore, a professional handyman and experienced joiner based in Barnoldswick. With over 25 years in the trade, I’ve built a reputation for reliable workmanship, honest advice, and high-quality finishes across a wide range of home improvements.

I’m ex-military, which means I understand the importance of punctuality, good communication, and completing work to a high standard. When you book me, you can expect a tidy, respectful service, clear timekeeping, and a job done properly.

My work is always carried out with care, and I take pride in my attention to detail. Whether you need a simple fix or a more detailed piece of bespoke carpentry, I approach every job with the same level of professionalism.
