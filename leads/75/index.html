Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you attend an emergency? expand_more
For emergencies in Greater London, we aim to be on-site within 60-90 minutes. Our rapid response team is stationed across the city to ensure maximum coverage 24/7.
Do you provide free, no-obligation quotes? expand_more
Yes, we offer free estimates for all planned works, including rewiring, lighting design, and EV installations. Emergency call-outs have a standard diagnostic fee which is explained upfront.
Are your electricians fully certified? expand_more
All our engineers are NICEIC approved, DBS checked, and carry public liability insurance. We provide full certification (EIC or EICR) upon completion of every project.
