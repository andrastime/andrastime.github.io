Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you fully insured for removals and goods in transit? expand_more

Yes, absolutely. We hold dedicated £50,000 Goods in Transit insurance covering items during handling and transit, plus £2,000,000 Public Liability insurance to safeguard your buildings, fixtures, stairwells, and common areas.

How are waste clearance fees calculated? expand_more

Unlike skip hire where you pay for fixed space regardless of use, we quote transparently based on the volume (1/4, 1/2, or full Luton load) and weight of items cleared. All commercial disposal charges and labor are included, and we provide an official Environment Agency Waste Transfer Note on completion.

Do you accommodate same-day or urgent short-notice bookings? expand_more

Yes, we keep dedicated support slots open daily for urgent relocations, landlord eviction clearances, emergency furniture collections, or unexpected tenancy deadlines. Call us directly on 01223 852 940 for instant dispatch.

What van sizes do you operate across Cambridge? expand_more

Our fleet features high-capacity 3.5-Tonne Luton Box vans fitted with hydraulic tail-lifts (carrying up to 750 cu. ft - equivalent to a standard 2-bedroom home), alongside Long Wheelbase (LWB) and Medium Wheelbase Transits for nimble urban single items and narrow Cambridge street access.
