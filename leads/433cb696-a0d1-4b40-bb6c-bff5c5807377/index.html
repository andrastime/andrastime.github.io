The Face Behind the Business

Bas's Story

Years working in schools taught me one critical thing: you have to think on your feet. When something breaks in a busy school, you don't always have the luxury of calling a specialist—you solve the problem right then and there.

I bring that same "can-do" problem-solving approach to every home I visit in Walsall. Whether it's a messy job others avoid or a tricky fix that requires creative thinking, I'm here to help.

I'm not just a contractor; I'm your neighbor. My goal is to make a real difference in our community through quality work and a level of honesty that's hard to find these days.

Common Questions

What is the best way to contact you? expand_more
WhatsApp is often the easiest way to contact me as photos can be displayed easily but I'm happy with a text or a call. Just snap a photo of the job and I can usually give you an idea of cost immediately.
What do you charge? expand_more
I charge for parts at cost and am upfront with these. For labor, on most small jobs you decide what the work is worth once it's completed. For larger projects, I'll provide a clear, fixed quote beforehand.
What jobs are you qualified to do? expand_more
As a handyman, I'm used to taking on all sorts of jobs. Years of working in schools taught me to think on my feet and fix problems quickly. Experience is the best teacher, and problem-solving is a big part of what I do—especially when others might avoid certain tasks.
