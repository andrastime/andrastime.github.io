How does your pricing and billing work?

expand_more

We provide transparent hourly rates starting at $80-$95/hour for standard tasks, or flat upfront quotes for clearly scoped projects. You will always approve an itemized digital estimate before work commences. No surprise charges.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

expand_more

Yes. Every RAA Maintenance craftsperson is backed by our active state license (#LIC-994821-B), covered by a comprehensive $2,000,000 commercial liability policy, and full workers' compensation coverage.

How quickly can you dispatch someone to my property?

expand_more

For urgent repairs (water leaks, power drops, security breaches), our regional dispatch units arrive in an average of 15 to 45 minutes. Routine repairs can be scheduled online with exact 2-hour arrival windows.

What does your 1-year craft warranty cover?

expand_more

Every repair or installation completed by RAA is covered for 365 days. If a fitting, door alignment, or fastener loosens or fails under normal use, we return and rectify it at zero charge to you.
