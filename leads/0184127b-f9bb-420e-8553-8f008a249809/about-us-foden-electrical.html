Founded in 2023, Foden Electrical Contracting Ltd was established with a singular mission: to provide the Midlands with a Tier-1 electrical service that bridges the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern technical standards.

Based in the heart of Redditch at the East Moons Moat Industrial Estate, our location allows us to respond rapidly to the needs of local businesses and homeowners alike. Whether it's a critical industrial machine supply or a bespoke domestic rewire, our approach remains the same—precision, efficiency, and a friendly, professional service.
