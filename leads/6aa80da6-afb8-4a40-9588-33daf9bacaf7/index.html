Our Story

ASK DAD was founded on a simple premise: to fill a gap in the construction market for high-quality, reliable service on smaller projects. Many larger firms shy away from these jobs, finding them unprofitable.

We embrace them. We understand that a leaking valve, a sticking exterior door, unaligned cupboards, or a cracked ceiling drywall repair is crucial to your daily peace of mind. Our team employs seasoned, fully vetted tradesmen who take immense pride in every repair.
