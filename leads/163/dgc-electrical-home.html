About DGC Electrical Solutions Limited

DGC Electrical Solutions Limited is a Napit approved, registered and fully insured electrical company covering Solihull and the surrounding areas. Dan set up DGC Electrical in 2019 after 15 years working on domestic, commercial and industrial electrical projects across the UK.

We offer a range of electrical services in both domestic and commercial properties and are dedicated to providing you with the highest standard of electrical service we possibly can. Whether you are looking for LED lighting upgrades or a new socket at home, no job is too small.
