Expert Logistics FAQs

Do I need to provide a parking space?

expand_more
No. That’s the Greenworkforce advantage. We arrive on customized heavy-duty cargo bicycles. We don’t need a permit, we don’t need a space, and we never charge you for parking fines or congestion fees.

How do you carry materials on a bike?

expand_more
Our specialized cargo bikes can carry up to 150kg of tools and materials. For most jobs, we carry everything required. For very large items (like sheets of plywood), we can arrange delivery to your site ahead of our arrival.

What is your minimum call-out?

expand_more
Our minimum booking is one hour at £75. After the first hour, we charge in 30-minute increments. This ensures you only pay for the time our technician is actually working on your project.

Are you insured and qualified?

expand_more
Absolutely. All Greenworkforce technicians have a minimum of 5 years experience in their trade and carry full Public Liability Insurance. We pride ourselves on precision engineering and clean execution.
