Birmingham Native

Built on the Foundation of Birmingham.

AR Electrical (Midlands) Ltd has been providing electrical services to both domestic and commercial customers in Birmingham and beyond for a number of years. Born from a desire to bridge the gap between "local handymen" and high-end engineering firms, our founder envisioned a company that combined the approachability of a local trade with the rigor of a corporate safety division.

Starting as a small team focused on domestic rewiring and safety inspections, we quickly earned a reputation for excellent work quality and professional working practices. Our growth into the commercial sector was fueled by this reputation, allowing us to handle larger contracts across the West Midlands, from office refits to specialized data cabling projects.

Today, we stand as a leading NICEIC Approved Contractor, serving the heart of Birmingham with a team that values every project's integrity, regardless of size. We remain deeply rooted in our community, ensuring that every home and business we touch is powered with reliability and precision.
