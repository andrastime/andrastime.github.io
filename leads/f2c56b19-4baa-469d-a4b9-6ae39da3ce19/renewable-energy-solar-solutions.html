Frequently Asked Questions

Do commercial solar panels require planning permission?

expand_more
In most cases, commercial solar installations fall under "Permitted Development" and do not require planning permission. However, restrictions may apply to listed buildings or conservation areas. We handle the entire feasibility study as part of our consultation.

What is the typical ROI for an industrial solar project?

expand_more
Most commercial clients see a full Return on Investment within 5 to 7 years. With electricity prices continuing to rise, this window is shortening. The system lifespan is typically 25+ years, providing nearly two decades of essentially free energy.

Can you install solar without stopping our operations?

expand_more
Yes. We specialize in live high-spec environments. Our project management team coordinates installations to ensure minimal impact on daily logistics, production cycles, or data center uptime.
