Pricing Insights

Clear answers to our most common billing questions.

Do you charge for travel time? expand_more

No. Unlike many London competitors, our timer only starts once we arrive at your premises. We do not charge for the time spent in London traffic.

Is VAT included in your quotes? expand_more

All standard rates mentioned are subject to VAT at the current prevailing rate. We provide clear VAT-itemised invoices for your records.

What is an EICR and why is it fixed price? expand_more

Electrical Installation Condition Reports (EICR) are formal safety tests. We offer fixed pricing based on the number of circuits to ensure you have predictable compliance costs.

How can I pay my invoice? expand_more

We accept all major credit/debit cards via secure mobile terminals on-site, as well as bank transfers (BACS) for larger project milestones.
