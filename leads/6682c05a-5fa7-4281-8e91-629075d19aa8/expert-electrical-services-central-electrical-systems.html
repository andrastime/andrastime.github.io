Frequently Asked Questions

How often should an EICR be carried out?

For commercial premises, typically every 5 years or upon change of tenancy. Residential properties are recommended every 10 years or at the point of sale.

Are you NICEIC Approved?

Yes, Central Electrical Systems Ltd is a fully approved NICEIC contractor, ensuring all work meets the highest industry standards and safety regulations.

Do you provide emergency 24/7 services?

We offer priority maintenance and repair services for our regular commercial and industrial clients, as well as rapid response for domestic emergencies.
