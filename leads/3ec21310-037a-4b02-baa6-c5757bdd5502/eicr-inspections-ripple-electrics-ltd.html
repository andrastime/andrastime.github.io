Landlord FAQ

help How often is an EICR required?

For rental properties, a new EICR is required every 5 years or at the start of a new tenancy. Some commercial properties may require more frequent testing.

help What happens if the report is 'Unsatisfactory'?

If faults are found, they will be coded C1 (Danger), C2 (Potentially Dangerous), or C3 (Improvement Recommended). Landlords must address C1 and C2 issues within 28 days to remain compliant.

Yes, we carry full public liability and professional indemnity insurance, backed by our JIB Gold Card and NAPIT certifications.
