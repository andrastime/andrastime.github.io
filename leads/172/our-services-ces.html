Expert Solutions for Every Sector.

From architectural super-prime residential projects to large-scale commercial retail hubs, CES delivers industry-leading electrical installations across the UK.

01 / Commercial Excellence

Commercial & Retail Installations

At CES, we specialize in high-capacity electrical frameworks for the modern commercial landscape. Our expertise covers full-scale retail fit-outs, office complexes, and industrial distribution centers. We understand that business continuity is paramount; hence our projects are delivered with surgical precision, meeting rigid timelines and rigorous safety standards like NICEIC and BAFE. Our integrated solutions include smart lighting, power distribution, and comprehensive fire safety systems designed for high-traffic environments.

02 / Residential Luxury

Super Prime Residential

For the world’s most prestigious homes, we provide bespoke electrical design and installation services that marry aesthetic elegance with technical sophistication. Our "Super Prime" team works alongside leading architects and interior designers to integrate invisible technology, high-end automation, and mood-setting lighting control systems (Lutron, Crestron). We deliver electrical infrastructure that is as refined as the architecture it serves, ensuring that every wire, plate, and sensor is placed with absolute consideration for the property's design integrity.

03 / Experience Focused

Hospitality & Leisure

In the hospitality sector, ambiance is everything. CES has a proven track record of delivering complex electrical installations for luxury hotels, high-end restaurants, and private member clubs. We specialize in dynamic lighting solutions, audio-visual integration, and reliable backend power systems that ensure seamless guest experiences. Our work prioritizes both functional safety and atmospheric design, creating environments that captivate and perform under the demands of 24/7 commercial operation.
