A Foundation of Trust

Est. 1996

Established in 1996, Countrywide Electrical Services Ltd began as a family-run business with a singular focus: delivering technical mastery without compromise. Over the decades, we have evolved from a local contractor into a nationally respected leader in the electrical engineering sector.

Our growth has been defined by a commitment to the "Gold-standard" of service. We specialize in complex, high-stakes environments—from luxury hospitality and retail flagships to heavy industrial infrastructure. Our reputation is built on the foundation of being a family-run business that treats every project with the meticulous attention it deserves, ensuring safety and precision are never secondary concerns.
