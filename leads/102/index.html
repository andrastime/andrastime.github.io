Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do in a plumbing emergency? expand_more
First, turn off your main water stopcock immediately to prevent further damage. Then, call our 24/7 emergency line at 020 8000 0000. Our dispatcher will guide you through safety steps while an engineer is sent to your location.
Do you provide free quotes? expand_more
Yes, we provide no-obligation free quotes for all major installations and planned work. For emergency repairs, we provide an upfront estimate over the phone and confirm pricing once the engineer assesses the situation on-site.
Are your engineers Gas Safe registered? expand_more
Absolutely. Every heating engineer at Plumb London is Gas Safe registered and undergoes regular training to stay up to date with the latest safety standards and technology.
