FAQS

Expert Answers to
Your Questions

How soon can you provide a quote?

We typically respond to online requests within 24 hours. For most domestic works, we can provide a ballpark estimate over the phone or email, followed by a formal onsite quote if required.

Are you NICEIC Approved?

Yes, AR Electrical (Midlands) Ltd is a fully NICEIC Approved Contractor. This means our work is regularly assessed to ensure it meets the highest national safety standards.

Do you cover commercial properties?

Absolutely. We handle everything from office LED lighting upgrades and data cabling to full warehouse electrical inspections and maintenance contracts.

What is an EICR and do I need one?

An Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR) is a formal safety check. It's mandatory for landlords every 5 years and highly recommended for homeowners to ensure safety.
