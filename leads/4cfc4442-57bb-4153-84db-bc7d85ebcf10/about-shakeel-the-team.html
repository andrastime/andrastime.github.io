The Journey of City Electrical Services

Founded in 2009, City Electrical Services was born out of a simple observation: the Midlands needed a contractor who combined technical mastery with a "clean-as-you-go" domestic standard. Shakeel, our founder, didn't want to just run a company from an office; he wanted to lead from the front.

"When you hire City Electrical Services, you get me. I'm the one answering the phone, the one on the ladders, and the one ensuring every connection is tightened to spec. I believe in straight talk—no hidden fees, no jargon, just honest engineering."

With over two decades of experience across domestic, commercial, and industrial sectors, Shakeel has seen it all—from the complex rewiring of period properties to the installation of modern smart home systems. Being 'on the tools' every day means he maintains a direct line of accountability with every client.
