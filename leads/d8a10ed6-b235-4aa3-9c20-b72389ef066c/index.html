Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you respond to an emergency? expand_more
We offer 24/7 emergency call-out services across East London. Typically, we can be on-site within 1-2 hours depending on your exact location in Stratford or surrounding areas.
Are all your services covered by a warranty? expand_more
Yes, all our workmanship is backed by a comprehensive 12-month warranty. We take pride in our "done properly the first time" approach, but we’re here to support you if anything needs adjustment.
Do you work with landlords and agencies? expand_more
Absolutely. We specialize in property management maintenance, helping landlords keep their portfolios tenant-ready and safe. We can handle everything from gas safety checks to end-of-tenancy refurbishments.
Can you provide a free quote? expand_more
Yes, we offer free, no-obligation quotes for all our services. We believe in transparent, fair pricing with no hidden surprises. Just give us a call or send an email with your project details.
