Why Professional Certification Matters

In the modern property market, electrical certification is no longer optional—it is a critical legal and safety requirement. For landlords, failing to provide a valid EICR can lead to substantial fines and invalidates your insurance. For home buyers, it is the only way to ensure the property you are purchasing doesn't have costly, dangerous wiring faults hidden behind the walls.
