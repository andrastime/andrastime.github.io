Frequently Asked Questions

Electrical Contracting FAQs

How fast can an emergency electrician arrive at my property? expand_more

Our average emergency response time is under 45 minutes across the tri-state area. We maintain multiple fully stocked fleet units stationed strategically near major parkways 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Do you handle utility coordination and municipal permits? expand_more

Yes, turnkey permitting is built into every project quote. For 200A/400A panel upgrades and commercial switchgear work, our licensed expediters file directly with the city building department and coordinate temporary shutoffs with the local utility company.

Are EV charging installations eligible for state tax credits and rebates? expand_more

Absolutely. Both residential Level 2 chargers and commercial fleet hubs frequently qualify for state clean energy rebates and federal alternative fuel infrastructure credits (up to 30%). We supply all stamped contractor documentation required for reimbursement.

What warranty do you provide on residential and commercial labor? expand_more

Every electrical installation performed by BBElectrical Contracting Ltd. is backed by our full 10-Year Bonded Craftsmanship Warranty, in addition to manufacturer product warranties (typically 10-25 years for panels and equipment).
